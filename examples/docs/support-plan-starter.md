# Kaikeel Handbook Lite: Starter Support Plan

*Fictional sample document for the DocQA demo. Prices are invented for illustration.*

The Starter plan is a monthly support plan for small projects after the warranty period ends.

## What the Starter plan includes

- Up to 4 hours of maintenance work per month (bug fixes, dependency updates, small tweaks).
- Monthly dependency and security update for one repository.
- Email support with a response within two business days.
- A short monthly report listing the work done.

Unused hours do not roll over to the next month.

## Price

The Starter plan costs USD 190 per month, billed monthly in advance. It can be cancelled at
any time with 14 days' notice.

## Who it is for

Brochure websites, internal tools and small automation scripts that change rarely and do not
need a fast response.
