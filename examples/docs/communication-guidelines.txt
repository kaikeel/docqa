Kaikeel Handbook Lite: Client Communication Guidelines
(Fictional sample document for the DocQA demo.)

RESPONSE TIMES
- Client messages are answered within one business day, even if the answer is only
  "received, we will reply with details by Thursday".
- Business days are Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 18:00 in the client's time zone agreed at
  kickoff.

LANGUAGE AND TONE
- Write to clients in English unless the client writes in another language first.
- Be calm and precise. State facts, dates and next steps. Avoid hype words and emoji.
- Never promise a number that has not been measured, such as a speed-up or an accuracy
  figure.

STATUS UPDATES
- Active projects receive a short written status update every Tuesday and Friday:
  what was done, what is next, and any blockers or decisions needed from the client.

MEETINGS
- Meetings are optional; written updates are the default. When a call is useful, it has an
  agenda sent in advance and a written summary sent within 24 hours afterwards.

APPROVALS
- Anything that commits the company, such as a quote, a scope change or a delivery, is
  reviewed and sent by the founder personally.
