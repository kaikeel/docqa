Kaikeel Handbook Lite: Pro Support Plan
(Fictional sample document for the DocQA demo. Prices are invented for illustration.)

The Pro plan is a monthly support plan for business-critical projects after the warranty
period ends.

WHAT THE PRO PLAN INCLUDES
- Up to 12 hours of maintenance and small feature work per month.
- Weekly dependency and security updates for up to three repositories.
- Priority support by email and chat with a response within four business hours.
- Uptime monitoring with alerts, and a SEV1 first response within 30 minutes.
- A monthly 30-minute review call and written report.

Unused hours roll over for one month, up to a maximum of 6 hours.

PRICE
The Pro plan costs USD 690 per month, billed monthly in advance, with a minimum term of
three months.

WHO IT IS FOR
Online shops, customer-facing APIs and AI assistants where downtime directly costs money.
