# Kaikeel Handbook Lite: Estimates and Change Requests

*Fictional sample document for the DocQA demo.*

## How we estimate

Work is broken into tasks of at most two hours each. The project manager adds the task
estimates, then adds a 20 percent buffer for review, testing and communication. We only
quote a delivery date after this breakdown exists; we never promise a deadline that has not
been estimated.

## Milestones

Projects larger than three days are split into milestones. Each milestone has its own
deliverable, acceptance criteria and price. A milestone is invoiced only after the client
accepts its deliverable.

## Change requests

Anything outside the agreed scope is a change request. The process is:

1. The client describes the change in writing.
2. We reply with an impact note: extra tasks, extra cost and the effect on the delivery
   date.
3. Work on the change starts only after the client approves the impact note in writing.

Small clarifications that do not add work (for example renaming a label) are not change
requests and are handled immediately.

## Hourly work

Maintenance after the warranty period is billed hourly in 30-minute increments, with a
weekly time report sent every Monday.
